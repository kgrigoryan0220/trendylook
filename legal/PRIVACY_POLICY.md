## 5. How We Use Your Information

We use information to:

1. **Provide the service** — authenticate you, analyze outfit photos, show scores and recommendations, save your check history, and enable sharing of results.
2. **Manage subscriptions** — verify Pro access, apply promo codes, restore purchases, and handle billing status.
3. **Improve reliability and product quality** — monitor crashes, fix bugs, and understand feature usage through analytics.
4. **Protect the service** — enforce rate limits, prevent abuse, and secure accounts.
5. **Comply with law** — respond to lawful requests and meet legal obligations.
6. **Communicate with you** — respond to support requests.

### 5.1 AI processing of photos

When you submit a photo for analysis:

1. The photo is uploaded to our **private, access-controlled storage** (Supabase Storage, bucket `look-photos`).
2. Our server generates a **time-limited signed URL** and sends the image to **OpenAI**'s API for automated outfit analysis.
3. The AI returns structured results (scores, recommendations, text), which we store in our database linked to your account.

**Important:**

- Analysis focuses on **clothing and styling**, not biometric identification.
- We do **not** use your photos for facial recognition or to identify you.
- Our AI instructions prohibit comments on weight, body type, or facial appearance.
- AI output is **automated** and for entertainment / informational purposes only (see Terms of Service).

According to OpenAI's API terms, data submitted through the API is generally **not used to train** their public models; OpenAI may retain API data for a limited period for abuse monitoring and service operation. See OpenAI's privacy policy for details.
